Privacy Policy

App: Zafir: Out of the Oasis
Developer: Games for Improving English (Zest English)
Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version

Zafir: Out of the Oasis does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or location.
  • No account or login details.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or cookies for tracking.
  • No photos, contacts, microphone or camera access.

What stays on your device

To remember your place in the story and your score, the game saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

How the game loads

The app displays the game from a web page hosted on GitHub Pages (davross99.github.io). Like any website, the hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as your IP address, when the page is loaded. We do not receive or use this information. You can read GitHub's privacy statement at GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other services

The game contains links to Google Play and to our website, www.zestenglish.art. These open outside the game and are covered by the privacy policies of those services.

Children

The app is made for English language learners, including young learners. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have any questions, please get in touch through our website: www.zestenglish.art.
