# ZAFIR: OUT OF THE OASIS — Game 1
### Full story with 100 gaps | Top 20 irregular verbs | B2 level | Journey structure

Zafir belongs to the Sahar, a small, easy-going community of spirit-creatures who live openly alongside humans, with no fixed duty or role. He leaves his home oasis out of simple curiosity, drawn by news of the Festival of Lights in the city of Qalhara. This chapter is the journey itself — the oasis, the desert crossing (with a sandstorm and a canyon rest along the way), and arrival at Qalhara, ending on a cliffhanger.

**Target verbs (20):** be, have, do, say, go, get, make, know, think, see, come, take, find, give, tell, feel, become, leave, put, mean

**Structure:** 5 waves × 20 gaps = 100, all 20 verbs once per wave. Past simple dominates narration; present tense and modal/infinitive constructions appear only inside direct speech or clearly non-past grammatical contexts.
